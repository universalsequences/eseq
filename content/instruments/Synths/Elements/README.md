# Elements

A port of Mutable Instruments **Elements**, the module's full voice:
- **Exciters:** bow, blow and strike, with the CONTOUR envelope. Blow includes the reed tube; strike includes the recorded samples, mallet, plectrum and particle modes.
- **Resonators:** the modal resonator (52 modes, as on the module) with its bowed waveguides, the module's two hidden string resonator models, and the "Ominous" easter-egg voice.
- **Part:** stereo spread, reverb and freeze, and the output soft clipping.

Ported from `elements/dsp` in https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack (commit
`f4cfa769`).

`dsp.lisp` and `data/` are generated by `tools/elements/regenerate.sh` from the
sources in `tools/elements`; edit those, not `dsp.lisp`. The data files are the
module's lookup tables, its strike samples and its blow noise recording,
copied verbatim from `resources.cc`.

## Models

`model` selects the resonator, as the module's hidden settings do:

- **Modal:** GEOMETRY goes from plate to string to bell.
- **String:** a Karplus-Strong string; GEOMETRY sets the curved bridge or the dispersion.
- **Strings:** five strings tuned to a chord picked by GEOMETRY.
- **Ominous:** the easter egg, two feedback-FM oscillator pairs through filters and rotating panners.
  - BLOW and STRIKE are the two oscillators: level, ratio and FM amount.
  - BOW is detune and feedback.
  - GEOMETRY is filter mode, BRIGHTNESS cut-off, DAMPING the envelope's filter amount, and POSITION the rotation.

`strength` is the PRESSURE input. Velocity lowers it by `vel_amt × (1 − velocity)`.
A trigger pulse while a note is held re-strikes it. Notes keep ringing after release until the resonator dies away.

## Fidelity

The port was checked sample by sample against the original C++ `Part`, compiled on the desktop and run at its native 32 kHz (`tools/elements/ref`). Both sides were fed the same random draws:

- **Exact (0.00% error over 1 s):**
  - all strike models, the bow, the blow (granular noise and tube) and the diffuser;
  - the envelope, the modal resonator and its bowed modes;
  - the string and chord-string models (every geometry, brightness and damping);
  - space, spread and freeze.
- **Small differences:**
  - Contour and bow cases differ by up to 0.2%: the bow's friction table amplifies float rounding.
  - Note and position sweeps differ by up to 0.6%.
  - The Ominous voice is within 0.1% over the first 100 ms and drifts slowly after that (float phases instead of 32-bit integers).
- **Reverb:** within 0.1% over the first 100 ms. Its modulated tail decorrelates over a second or two, from float rounding in the LFO, while its level and spectrum still match.
- **Random paths:** with real noise, the particle, bow and blow envelopes and spectra match statistically.

At other sample rates the voice keeps the module's 32 kHz behaviour in time and frequency:
- every filter keeps its analog cut-off;
- delay lines keep their lengths in seconds;
- decays keep their times;
- per-block work runs on 0.5 ms blocks;
- impulse and noise exciters are rescaled to the same energy.

Against the resampled 32 kHz module, the 48 kHz instrument is within 0.3 dB in level, with 2–7% waveform error.

Deliberate differences:

- **Note starts:** a new note starts from settled smoothers (strength, pickup position, string interpolators), as on the running module. A freshly powered-on module would start from zero.
- **Seed:** the per-unit serial-number seed is not applied. The module's unseeded defaults are used:
  - side LFO 0.5 Hz, offset 0.1;
  - reverb diffusion 0.625, reverb low-pass 0.7;
  - exciter signature 0.
- **No smoothing:** no panel-pot smoothing and no pitch slew. Freeze is a switch rather than SPACE CV beyond 1.75.
- **Mode count:** the modal mode count assumes the partials rise with the index. The only exception is at GEOMETRY below 2/256 for notes above about 2.4 kHz.
- **No panic:** the resonator panic reset (on a runaway level) is not ported.
- **Loop filters:** the tube's and the string's one-sample loop filters run at the host rate, so they are slightly brighter above 32 kHz.
- **Output gain:** the outputs are the module's L/R jacks after its soft clipper. `level_db` adds to a makeup gain of +12 dB for the resonator models (0 dB for Ominous), so the models sit at similar loudness.

## CPU

About 9–11% of a core per voice at 48 kHz, or about 17% with the Ominous voice selected.
- **What runs all the time:** the modal resonator's 52 modes recompute their coefficients every sample (DGen cannot yet run block-rate math once per block). The string models' delay lines run whichever model is selected.
- **What is skipped:** only the Ominous voice is skipped when not selected.
