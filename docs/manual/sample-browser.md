# Samples and sounds

- **Samples** are audio files for a sampler.
- **Sounds** are saved track setups.
- **Kits** are saved drum racks.
- **Instruments** are generators and rack types.
- **Presets** are settings for the selected instrument.

## Find a sample

Open **Samples** and search, or click tag chips such as kick, snare, or a collection name. Factory and Yours split shipped and personal collections.

Selecting a sample shows its waveform. The headphone button auditions samples as you move through them.

![The Samples browser combines collection tags, a file list, and waveform preview.](images/sample-browser.png)

## Load a sampler

Drag a sample onto **Drop sounds here** for a new track, or onto a track to replace its sound. Dropping a sample on a synth track turns it into a sampler.

Enter a step and play. Transpose changes pitch; the sampler's envelope and playback controls shape the hit.

## Import your own

1. Drag audio files or a folder from Finder onto the window.
2. Review the staged tree: ready, already in the library, and failed.
3. Add a batch tag for the whole import, such as the collection name.
4. Select a folder or file to give it extra tags or a title.
5. Click **Import**.

![Review the staged files and their tags before importing.](images/sample-import.png)

Importing adds files to the library. Loading is a separate step. For several drums, build a [Drum Rack](racks) rather than replacing one sampler repeatedly.

## Sounds and kits

Save a track's setup as a Sound, or a drum rack as a Kit, to reuse it in other projects. Drag a preset onto the Sounds tab to promote it to a Sound. Loading a Sound or Kit replaces what is on the selected track.
